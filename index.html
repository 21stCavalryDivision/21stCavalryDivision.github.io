WHO WE ARE

Built for teamwork.
Driven by standards.

The 21st Cavalry Division is an organized military simulation community focused on coordinated, immersive operations while maintaining a welcoming environment for its members.

Every member has a purpose. Ground, armor, aviation, reconnaissance, medical, support, and leadership elements work together as one force.

PROJECT MISSION

Develop capable members who can adapt to any mission and operate effectively as one team.

The mission of the 21st Cavalry Division is to build a disciplined, organized, and professional unit focused on teamwork, leadership, and tactical excellence. Through structured training, realistic operations, and strong unit cohesion, we aim to develop confident members prepared to succeed in any assigned role.

UNIT CAPABILITIES

One force. Multiple roles.

01

Dismounted and mechanized maneuver elements focused on coordinated team operations.
