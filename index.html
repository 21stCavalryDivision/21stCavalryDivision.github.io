WHO WE ARE

Built for teamwork.
Driven by standards.

The 21st Cavalry Division is an organized military simulation community focused on creating coordinated, immersive operations while maintaining a welcoming environment for its members.

Our structure is designed to give every member a purpose. Whether operating on the ground, supporting armored elements, flying aviation missions, or leading troops, every role contributes to the mission.

PROJECT MISSION

Develop capable members who can adapt to any mission and operate effectively as one team.

The mission of the 21st Cavalry Division is to build a disciplined, organized, and professional unit focused on teamwork, leadership, and tactical excellence. Through structured training, realistic operations, and strong unit cohesion, we aim to develop confident members prepared to succeed in any assigned role.

TRAINING PIPELINE

Train with purpose.
