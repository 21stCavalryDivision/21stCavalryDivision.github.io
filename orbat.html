03 // SPECIALIZED ELEMENTS

Combat Support

Attached Capabilities
01

Aviation Detachment

Pilots, copilots and crew chiefs supporting transport, reconnaissance and air-mobile operations.

02

Armor Section

Vehicle commanders, gunners, drivers and crewmen providing armored mobility and direct fire support.

03

Recon Section

Forward reconnaissance, observation and information collection in support of the maneuver force.

04

Medical Section

Combat medical personnel responsible for casualty response and battlefield medical support.

05

Training Section

Instructors and training NCOs responsible for BCT, qualifications, standards and readiness.

06

Recruitment Section

Recruiters responsible for applicant processing, onboarding and integration into the division.

07

Command Staff

Administrative and operational staff supporting planning, accountability and division-level coordination.

08

Unassigned Personnel

Members awaiting permanent assignment, billet placement or completion of required training.
