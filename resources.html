SOP 1-01

General Unit Standards

Members are expected to maintain professionalism, follow the chain of command, communicate clearly, and contribute to a positive team environment. During organized operations, members follow assigned leadership and remain focused on the mission.

Core Expectations

  • Respect other members and staff.
  • Be prepared before scheduled training and operations.
  • Use the proper chain of command for unit matters.
  • Follow event-specific instructions and assigned roles.
  • Participate in after-action reviews constructively.

SOP 2-01

Communications

Unit communications should be brief, clear, and relevant. Operational channels are kept free of unnecessary traffic so leaders and teams can pass important information efficiently.

Standard

  • Identify who you are calling before transmitting your message.
  • Keep transmissions concise.
  • Acknowledge instructions when required.
  • Move extended discussion to an appropriate channel.

TRADOC 1-01

Training & Qualifications

All members complete initial orientation and basic training before advanced qualification. Specialized billets may require additional evaluation by qualified instructors.

ADMIN 1-01

Attendance

Members should make a reasonable effort to attend scheduled unit events and communicate when real-life obligations prevent participation. Consistent unexplained inactivity may result in reassignment to reserve status so active billets remain available.
