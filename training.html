TRAINING & QUALIFICATIONS

A structured progression from entry-level standards to specialized and leadership qualifications. Every training block is designed to build competent, dependable members who can operate effectively within the division.

01 // TRAINING PIPELINE

Path to Readiness

Progression System
01

Basic Combat Training

Unit standards, communications, movement, formations, safety, map skills, reporting procedures and team fundamentals.

02

Core Qualifications

Rifle qualification, CLS fundamentals and validation of the basic skills required for assignment to an operational element.

03

Role Training

Members train for their assigned billet in infantry, weapons, medical, communications, vehicles, aviation or support roles.

04

Advanced Readiness

Leadership, instructor and specialized qualification tracks build members capable of operating and leading under complex conditions.

04 // READINESS STANDARD

Mission Ready

Validation Criteria

Readiness Expectations

Training is not just a checklist. Members are expected to demonstrate the skills, communication and discipline required to perform their billet during unit operations.

Understands unit SOPs and communication standards
Demonstrates safe and competent weapon handling
Can operate within a team and follow leadership
Maintains required role qualifications
Participates in sustainment and refresher training
