TRAINING COMMAND

TRAINING PIPELINE

Progressive training designed to build confident members, qualified specialists, and capable leaders.

PHASE 01

Entry & Orientation

Community expectations, unit structure, attendance standards, communications setup, and introduction to unit procedures.

  • Discord and unit setup
  • Chain of command
  • Basic communications
  • Unit standards
PHASE 02

Basic Combat Training

Core skills required before assignment to an operational element.

  • Movement and formations
  • Map and navigation fundamentals
  • Radio procedure
  • Medical and casualty response basics
  • Team-level coordination
PHASE 03

Role Qualification

Specialized instruction based on the member's assigned billet.

  • Infantry / reconnaissance
  • Armor crew
  • Aviation crew
  • Medical / support
  • Leadership track
PHASE 04

Collective Training

Squad, troop, and combined-arms exercises focused on coordination and mission execution.

  • Element integration
  • Mission rehearsals
  • After-action reviews
  • Advanced qualifications

QUALIFICATION STANDARD

Train to the standard, not the clock.

Members progress when they demonstrate the required knowledge, communication, teamwork, and role competency. Training staff should document completed phases and qualifications on the unit roster.
